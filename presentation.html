Slide 2 · Overview

What is the Automatic Dialer?

??

Automated Calling

Reads your contact list and calls each person automatically at a specified date and time — no manual dialing needed.

?

Scheduled Precision

Each contact has an exact date and time. The dialer waits and only dials when the clock matches the schedule precisely.

??

IVR Audio Playback

When the customer answers, a pre-recorded voice message (IVR) is automatically played into the call.

??

Live Dashboard

Track every call in real-time: who was called, result (answered / no answer / failed), duration, and full logs.

Slide 3 · Workflow

How It Works

1

Upload Your Contact List

Import an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with customer names, phone numbers, scheduled date & time, and notes. The system reads each row automatically.

2

Configure & Start Auto-Dialer

Select your IVR audio, set pacing delay, then click Start Auto-Dialer. The system monitors the schedule queue every second.

3

Calls Are Made at Scheduled Time

When the clock hits the scheduled time, the dialer places a SIP call automatically. A live countdown timer shows exactly how long until the next call.

4

Track Results in Dashboard

Every call result (Completed, No Answer, Failed) is recorded with timestamp, duration, and response in the call log.

Understanding Call Statuses

StatusMeaningNext Action
PendingWaiting for scheduled time to arriveDialer will call automatically at scheduled time
CallingCall is currently being dialed / ringingWaiting for customer to answer
CompletedCustomer answered; IVR audio was playedLogged. Moves to next contact.
No AnswerCustomer did not pick up or line was busyLogged. Use Requeue to retry later.
